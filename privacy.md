WordWizard Privacy Policy
Effective date: October 2, 2026
WordWizard respects your privacy. WordWizard does not operate its own user-account system and does not sell personal information or use personal information for advertising.
Information collected by WordWizard
WordWizard does not transmit personal information to a WordWizard-operated server. Game progress, local high scores, settings, and other game data are stored locally on your device.
WordWizard does not collect location information, contacts, photos, messages, microphone recordings, advertising identifiers, or payment-card information.
Purchases
WordWizard offers an optional one-time Full Game purchase. Purchases are processed by Google Play. WordWizard does not receive or store your payment-card or banking information. Google processes purchases according to Google's own privacy policies and terms.
Google Play Games
WordWizard may use Google Play Games Services for optional platform features such as leaderboards. When these features are used, Google may process information associated with your Google Play Games profile and gameplay activity, such as your player identity, profile information made available by Google Play Games, and leaderboard scores.
WordWizard does not operate an independent online player-profile database.
Advertising and tracking
WordWizard contains no third-party advertising and does not use advertising trackers to build profiles for targeted advertising.
Local game data
Game progress, preferences, history, and other locally saved WordWizard information remain on the player's device unless a platform service explicitly provides a synchronization feature.
Removing WordWizard may remove locally stored game data unless it is preserved by an operating-system or platform backup service.
Children's privacy
WordWizard does not knowingly collect personal information from children through a WordWizard-operated service. Any Google platform services used by the game are governed by Google's applicable account, privacy, and parental-control policies.
Data retention and deletion
Because WordWizard does not maintain its own online user accounts or personal-data database, there is no WordWizard server account or associated personal information to delete. Locally stored WordWizard data can be removed by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the application.
Information processed by Google Play or Google Play Games is controlled through the applicable Google account and Google services.
Changes to this Privacy Policy
This policy may be updated if WordWizard's features or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
